About

Parra Macro is independent macro strategy research by Oscar Parra, covering rates, sovereign credit, emerging-market FX and commodities.

What we publish

  • Rates — a Fed policy-path model set against fed funds futures, plus emerging-market policy rates.
  • Country risk — sovereign default probabilities and internal 1–10 ratings for about 190 countries, with EM FX and carry screens.
  • Commodities — named-scenario forecasts for oil, gas, agriculture and metals.
  • Trade ideas — a weekly board of positions where our models and market pricing disagree.
  • Research — monthly notes on Substack.

The GeoRisk app

GeoRisk Monitor is our live geopolitical risk app. It runs thousands of news sources a day through NLP sentiment analysis, GDELT event monitoring and multi-source aggregation to produce composite risk scores across six indicators, for 190+ countries with priority tracking of 50 key nations. Scores update continuously through the trading day.

How we work

  • Model against market — every view starts from a model estimate compared with what prices imply; the trade is in the gap.
  • Data first — official statistics, market data and news signals, with the datasets behind each chart available on the Data page.
