Methodology

Model side. Per-country inertial Taylor rule: r_t = ρ·r_{t-1} + (1−ρ)·[α + β_π·(π − π*) + β_y·y_gap] + ε. Fit by OLS on 15-20 years of monthly policy-rate + CPI-YoY history. Sign constraints: β_π ≥ 0.5 (Taylor principle) or the term drops; β_y ≥ 0 or the term drops; ρ ∈ [0.5, 0.98].

Direction bucketing. gap = implied − current. If |gap| > 75bp → ±50bp call. If > 25bp → ±25bp call. Otherwise HOLD. Confidence: HIGH if |gap| > 50bp and r² > 0.85, MEDIUM if |gap| > 25bp and r² > 0.70, else LOW.

10Y direction. Not a fitted model — a heuristic that combines the policy-rate call with the 3M yield move: policy HIKE + yield up ≥ 30bp → HIGHER; policy CUT + yield down ≥ 30bp → LOWER; policy HOLD + |Δ3M| < 30bp → SIDEWAYS; else MIXED.

Data. Policy rates: FRED OECD / IMF policy rate series (primary), IMF IFS FIPR_PA (fallback). CPI YoY: FRED OECD CPALTT01 series or computed from IMF IFS PCPI_IX. 10Y yields: yfinance <iso2>10YT=X tickers, FRED IRLTLT01 fallback. Meeting calendars hardcoded annually against each central bank's published schedule.
