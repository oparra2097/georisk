Methodology

Market side. CME ZQ (fed funds) monthly contracts via Yahoo Finance, refreshed every 6 hours. Implied rate = 100 − settle_price. Each contract prices the AVERAGE effective fed funds rate over its contract month.

Model side. Reduced-form Engle-Granger–estimated inertial Taylor rule from the FRB/US-lite panel at /macro-model. Fed funds is one of 11 endogenous equations. Bands are bootstrap draws (p10 / p50 / p90).

FedWatch decomposition. Each ZQ contract's month-average is decomposed into pre-meeting and post-meeting daily rates via the calendar-day-weighted identity implied = (d_before/d_total)·pre_rate + (d_after/d_total)·post_rate. The delta between the post-meeting and pre-meeting rate is bucketed linearly across ±25 / hold / ±50 so the expected value of the bucketed distribution equals the observed delta. Standard CME methodology.

Edge. Model p50 at the meeting date (linear interpolation between quarter-end points) minus market post-rate. Positive → model expects higher, market is dovish, short duration. Negative → model expects lower, market is hawkish, long duration.
